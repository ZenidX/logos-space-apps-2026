## 1. Most important findings (campaign and text audit)
1. **IceBite link is wrong.** astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/drilling-into-arctic-ice/ (26 Sep 2011) is about the **IceBreaker** drill at **Haughton Crater (Arctic)**. It does not mention IceBite, University Valley or Antarctica. Use the NASA Astrobiology ASTEP page dated 22 Jul 2008 instead (row C11).
2. **JETT3 dates "3–11 Oct 2022" are not in any source.** NASA gives a test run on 4 Oct and moonwalks on 5, 6, 8 and 9 Oct, with the test wrapped up on 12 Oct. The preview said "planned Oct. 4-9".
3. **The "moonwalking-test-mission-daily-field-log" page is the JETT3 log (Oct 2022), not JETT5.** misiones.md cites it for JETT5.
4. **"Sun cart" is not the source's wording for JETT5.** USGS says "a portable spotlight acting as the Sun". NASA's JETT3 log does say "lighting cart".
5. **The site break texts in sites.json quote the wrong mission's ranges.** Craters of the Moon says "albedo outside (<= 0.5)" and Kīlauea Iki says "NDVI outside (<= 0.10)". Those are M3 ranges; M1's are 0.07–0.20 and ≤ 0.05.
6. **mdrs.marssociety.org now redirects to a fundraising page** (mars-desert-research-station.raiselysite.com, a JS-only app). It does not show "Hanksville" to a reader.
7. **README:8 lists missions the app does not have** ("building a base, operating solar panels"). **README:36 points to `docs/misiones.md`, which does not exist** (docs/ has `misiones-elegidas.md`).
8. **"South-pole crust is feldspathic"** can be sourced. NASA JSC "Houston We Have a Podcast" Ep. 236 (D. Kring) says anorthosite appears in the walls of Shackleton crater at the south pole and was spread around the south-polar region. Replacement text is in row P30.
