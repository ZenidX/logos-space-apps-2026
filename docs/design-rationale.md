## Notes
- Apple publishes no numeric durations or easing curves; ours follow the Material Design 3 tokens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency` and `prefers-contrast` are the web media queries we use for the corresponding user settings.
- Not verified by us: behaviour on a physical phone; the contrast figures are computed, not measured on every background frame of the 3D scene.
