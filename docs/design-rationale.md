## Notes
- Apple publishes no numeric durations or easing curves; ours follow the Material Design 3 tokens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency` and `prefers-contrast` are the web media queries we use for the corresponding user settings.
- Not verified by us: behaviour on a physical phone; the contrast figures are computed, not measured on every background frame of the 3D scene.

### What we took from where
| idea | seen in | how it shows in Logos |
|---|---|---|
| Links between places that never cut through a globe (their arcs rise with distance) | vasturiano/three-globe, shuding/cobe | We tried arcs and replaced them with the «shared orbit» scheme: the Moon and Mars travel on drawn orbits around the Earth and a straight line of light links a site to its target only when the line is clear (`src/lib/orbit-math.ts`, `laserAlpha`); written from scratch, with a test that no line passes through a globe |
| Atmosphere as a rim glow on a back-side sphere (power of the angle to the view) | vasturiano/three-globe, shuding/cobe | Same technique in our own shader in `OrbitScene.tsx` |
| Glass panels need a fallback: blur plus a thin luminous edge where refraction filters are not supported | tomacco/amazing-glass, rdev/liquid-glass-react, Z1Code/glass-refraction | We use `backdrop-filter` blur with a 1 px gradient edge only (no SVG refraction, which is Chromium-only), and solid panels on phones, without `backdrop-filter`, with reduced transparency or with more contrast |
| HUD panels floating over a full-screen globe, data as the protagonist | ptteo/united-states-of-git, graykode/starquake | Full-screen scene behind translucent panels; big figure plus small label, no nested boxes |
