# Origin of the idea

Recorded on **14 November 2026 (CET)**, during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Barcelona local event). English translation of the original Spanish note (`docs/es/origen-idea.md`, first committed at 21:31 CET, commit `d281f34`).

## Statement
- The idea of this project belongs to **zexu and the team (Logos)**:
  1. Look for Earth analogs **per mission**: not "which place on Earth looks like the Moon?" but "where can this specific task be rehearsed?" (robots and suits on sand or regolith-like ground, building a base, solar panels under the target's Sun angle, extracting water from ice…).
  2. Add a **geopolitical feasibility** layer, presented neutrally as "access complexity".
- zexu took this decision and gave it to the AI agents on the evening of 14 November 2026, when this repository was started.
- Which specific missions to use was **not decided** by the idea itself: real analog field campaigns were researched (sources in `docs/missions.md`) and four were chosen (three by data availability; the fourth, low-Sun operations, by zexu's decision of 15 Nov, from his original list), subject to the team's veto.

## Use of Super Intelligence (SI) / AI — as a tool only
- AI agents (Claude) were used to search and summarise information and datasets with sources, to write and review code, and to draft texts that the team reviews.
- AI was **not** used to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge.
- What the AI did is declared in `README.md`, `AI_USAGE.md` and on the project page. Nothing generated with AI carries NASA logos or marks; no AI-generated images or video are used.

## Decisions taken by zexu during the hackathon (recorded as they were made, CET)
- **15 Nov 2026, 00:36** — zexu rejected the "shared orbit" scheme (Moon and Mars drawn around the Earth) as untrue, because Mars does not orbit the Earth, and chose, among samples shown to him: (1) the scene as the **real system with the Sun** (heliocentric overview with orbit radii and periods in real proportion; close-up of the Earth with the target world beside it; an honest footer on what is and is not to scale); (2) the **radial interface** (the index in the centre, one ring per mission condition); (3) a fourth mission, **"Low-Sun operations and solar power"**, from his original list of 14 Nov ("solar panels according to the Sun angle"), and, only if data and validation can be confirmed in time, a fifth, "Building with local soil / landing pads" (his "building bases"); the remaining real campaigns go in a text-only "Next missions" section, not scored.
- **15 Nov 2026, 00:38** — zexu asked for sites on the Earth to be shown with semi-transparent dashed lines, in the same visual language as the orbits.
- **15 Nov 2026, 00:43** — zexu decided, after seeing three variants: (1) sites on the Earth are shown as **"lines to labels"**: a semi-transparent dashed line from each visible site to its name, placed outside the globe, and **no line to the target world**; the opened site is brighter and its dot pulses; names never overlap, hide with their site on the far side and come back when the globe is turned; on phones only the opened site and the first few. (2) Four additions made with data the project already had: **logistics filters** (reachable in under 6 h, remote over 48 h, low access complexity), a **Flat / Steep / Any** terrain selector with declared thresholds and the note that slope is not part of the index, **evidence levels** as the main badge and as the order of the list (used by agencies / new region with support nearby / partial / not checked), and one **"Hardware rehearsed"** line per mission with the campaign's real equipment, with sources only. Optional, if everything is stable: "Your priorities" sliders that re-rank the shortlist in the browser. (3) What not to do: no "fit %" or "fidelity" names for the index, no weights without a source, no missions without a real campaign; anything reused from another project must be cited.
- **15 Nov 2026, 01:22** — the independent check contradicted the volcanic rock assumed for the Tankwa Karoo region (mission 1). The check was run by AI agents with the sources listed in `docs/independent-check-v8.md`. Applying the team's standing rule that nothing unverified or contradicted is shown as a candidate, the region was removed and listed with its reason under "Removed" (subject to the team's veto).
- **15 Nov 2026, 01:55** — wording only: the top evidence level is shown as "Used in real campaigns" instead of "used by agencies", because one of the known sites (the Mars Desert Research Station) is run by the Mars Society, not by a space agency.
- Recorded at 00:47, 01:27 and 01:55 CET on 15 Nov 2026. AI agents implemented these decisions; they did not take them.
