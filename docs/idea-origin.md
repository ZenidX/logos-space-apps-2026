# Origin of the idea

Recorded on **14 November 2026 (CET)**, during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Barcelona local event). English translation of the original Spanish note (`docs/es/origen-idea.md`, first committed at 21:31 CET, commit `db61bf7`).

## Statement
- The idea of this project belongs to **zexu and the team (Logos)**:
  1. Look for Earth analogs **per mission**: not "which place on Earth looks like the Moon?" but "where can this specific task be rehearsed?" (robots and suits on sand or regolith-like ground, building a base, solar panels under the target's Sun angle, extracting water from ice…).
  2. Add a **geopolitical feasibility** layer, presented neutrally as "access complexity".
- zexu took this decision and gave it to the AI agents on the evening of 14 November 2026, when this repository was started.
- Which specific missions to use was **not decided** by the idea itself: real analog field campaigns were researched (sources in `docs/missions.md`) and three were chosen by data availability, subject to the team's veto.

## Use of Super Intelligence (SI) / AI — as a tool only
- AI agents (Claude) were used to search and summarise information and datasets with sources, to write and review code, and to draft texts that the team reviews.
- AI was **not** used to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge.
- What the AI did is declared in `README.md`, `AI_USAGE.md` and on the project page. Nothing generated with AI carries NASA logos or marks; no AI-generated images or video are used.
