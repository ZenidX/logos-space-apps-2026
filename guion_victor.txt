What if we could test the future of space exploration already here on Earth?

With TerraVista we combine nasa planetary and earth observation data to identify similar terrestrial environments to moon and mars, revealoing where similarities break. 

We combine their different characteristics, revealing their differences and helping researchers at nasa find the best place to test future technologies.

Furthemore we transform these locations into destinations for aerospace testing, universities and science tourism for local and american government economic growth
