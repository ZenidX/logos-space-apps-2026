Rehearse the Moon and Mars on Earth.

Before crews walk on the Moon or drive on Mars, they practise here: on lava fields, sand, frozen ground and under a low polar Sun.

The idea

Start from the mission, not the planet.

For each field task we score all land on Earth against one target, condition by condition, and say where the comparison breaks.

Mission 1 of 4 · Moon · Mons Mouton

Suited EVA on volcanic terrain

An operational analogue for suited traverses and field geology — not a match to Mons Mouton's highland rock.

Volcanic rock is mapped within ~80 km (0.5° geological map); basalt at the site itself is not confirmed.

Mission 2 of 4 · Mars · Jezero crater

Rover on sand

Loose, sandy, bare ground where rovers can get stuck — and learn not to.

Sand fraction is a soil-model estimate with a wide uncertainty; it indicates sandy soil, not dunes.

Mission 3 of 4 · Mars · Arcadia Planitia

Water from ground ice

Cold, dry regions to screen for possible near-surface ice; the score does not detect ice.

Ground ice is inferred from temperature and dryness; no ice was detected directly.

Mission 4 of 4 · Moon · Mons Mouton

Low-Sun operations and solar power

Bare ground where the noon Sun stays near the horizon for months, as it does all year at the lunar south pole.

Computed, not validated: no field campaign has rehearsed this under a real low Sun, so there is no known site to check the index against. The Sun's height comes from latitude only.

How it works
  1. 1
    Start from a real mission

    We read what NASA, ESA and Mars Society field campaigns actually rehearsed, and turned each task into a few surface conditions that open data can measure.

  2. 2
    Score all land on Earth

    NASA MODIS and GPM grids, soil and rock maps: every 0.1° cell gets an analog index per mission. Known analog sites are used to check that the index finds them; where no campaign site exists, the mission says “computed, not validated”.

  3. 3
    Show new regions — and their limits

    Each mission gets new regions to investigate, compared with its target condition by condition, with where the analogy breaks and how complex access is.

Other real campaigns

Analog campaigns that have already run and that we have not scored: they are not driven by terrain, or there is no global data to compare. Listed for context.

  • CAVES · ESA · not scored
    Astronaut teams working underground in Sardinian caves, a stand-in for lava tubes (2011–2019).
  • ARCHES · DLR, ESA, KIT · not scored
    A lander, two rovers and a drone working together on Mount Etna, with a rover driven remotely (2022).
  • PISCES / ACME landing pad · NASA KSC Swamp Works, PISCES · not scored
    A teleoperated rover laid 100 sintered basalt pavers to build a landing pad in Hawai'i (2015).
  • NEEMO · NASA · not scored
    Spacewalk operations and isolation in the Aquarius undersea station, Florida (23 missions, 2001–2019).
  • CHAPEA · NASA · not scored
    Year-long simulated Mars surface stays inside a 3D-printed habitat at Johnson Space Center.
  • AMADEE · Austrian Space Forum · not scored
    Full Mars surface simulations with suits, rovers and a habitat (Oman 2018, Israel 2021, Armenia 2024).
